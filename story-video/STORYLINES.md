# Storylines — «الظل»

Source: narration `assets/narration.mp3` (3:21, Arabic). The voice tells the whole story; the picture never repeats a sentence word for word.
Angels are shown as light only — no faces or figures.

## ★ C — خيال الظل (recommended: the story is about a shadow, and shadow theatre is an Arab art form)

- **Device / form:** a character does the work · one continuous take across a shadow-puppet stage.
- **What the viewer sees:** a backlit cloth screen. Cut-paper silhouettes act out his life after him: the wife with the baby, the boy growing taller in jumps, a school, two weddings. In the bottom corner one puppet never moves: a bent woman. As time speeds up the other puppets blur past and she stays. At the end the camera goes behind the cloth and finds her real shadow, still crying.
- **First 2 s:** a lamp flickers on behind the cloth; a man's silhouette lies down and his shadow lifts off the cloth upward into white light.
- **Beats:** death and pleading (light) → the angel turns on the cloth → life races past as puppets → the fixed shadow in the corner → time slows to earth time → the reveal «سوى أمي» → hold on her alone.
- **Look:** warm amber cloth on near-black; ink-black paper silhouettes with visible cut edges and pin joints; Arabic captions in Amiri.
- **Sound:** the narration, a low drone, a clock that ticks faster with the years, and silence for the last line.

## A — الشاشة

- **Device / form:** the result first · an endless zoom.
- **What the viewer sees:** a white, fogged space. An old television floats in it, and his family's life plays on the screen as flickering home footage drawn in lines. Each zoom dives into the screen into the next year. A dark smudge sits in the same spot of every frame.
- **First 2 s:** the TV flickers on in the white fog, showing a woman holding a baby.
- **Beats:** the white space → the TV → zoom year after year → the smudge → zoom into the smudge → mother.
- **Look:** milky white fog, grey line drawings, one black stain; Arabic captions in Amiri.
- **Sound:** narration, TV static, a slow heartbeat.

## B — الزمن

- **Device / form:** type carries the argument · cut on the beat.
- **What the viewer sees:** giant Arabic words fill the vertical frame: «دقيقتين», «أيام», «سنوات». A year counter races 1 → 15. Between the words sits one black ink drop that never moves. At the end every word falls away and the drop becomes the word «أمي».
- **First 2 s:** «لقد توفيت منذ دقيقتين» stamped on black, the word «دقيقتين» in red.
- **Beats:** the death line → the time rule → years racing → the drop that stays → the drop becomes «أمي».
- **Look:** black and paper-white, one red accent, heavy Arabic type.
- **Sound:** narration, cuts on the clock ticks.
